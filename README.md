# Kiosk of Confidence

### Practice first. Use technology independently.

Kiosk of Confidence is an interactive digital learning platform designed to help older adults and people with limited digital experience become more confident when using everyday self-service machines.

The platform provides a safe environment where users can understand, practise, and gradually learn to complete common self-service tasks independently.

## The Problem

Self-service machines are increasingly common in banks, railway stations, restaurants, and other public places. However, older adults and technology beginners may find unfamiliar interfaces, buttons, and multiple steps confusing.

This can make everyday activities stressful and create unnecessary dependence on others.

## Our Solution

Kiosk of Confidence allows users to practise using simulated self-service machines without the fear of making real-world mistakes.

### Our Three Simulators

**1. ATM Simulator**
- Practise selecting basic banking services.
- Enter fictional transaction amounts.
- Review and edit selections.
- View a simulated transaction receipt.

**2. Ticket Booking Simulator**
- Select departure and destination.
- Choose ticket class and journey type.
- Select the number of passengers.
- View a simulated ticket.

**3. Food Ordering Simulator**
- Select dine-in or takeaway.
- Explore food categories and menu items.
- Adjust quantities.
- Review the order and view a simulated receipt or token.

## Our Main Feature: Three Learning Modes

Our platform is designed around a learning journey:

- **Learn:** Understand the purpose of buttons and the steps involved.
- **Guided Practice:** Complete tasks with instructions and helpful hints.
- **Independent Practice:** Repeat tasks with fewer hints and make decisions independently.

Our goal is to help users move from understanding to practice and eventually to independent use.

## Accessibility Features

- Large and readable text.
- Touch-friendly controls.
- English and Urdu language options.
- Browser-based text-to-speech support, where available.
- Help panel and explanations.
- Progress indicators.
- Review and edit options.

## Known Limitations

This is an educational prototype. It demonstrates self-service workflows rather than connecting to actual service providers.

- ATM transactions and balances are fictional.
- Ticket bookings are simulated and do not reserve real tickets.
- Food orders are simulated and are not sent to restaurants.
- No real payments or banking operations are performed.
- Speech and Urdu voice availability depend on browser and device support.
- Real-world service integrations and large-scale user testing are future possibilities.

## Privacy and Safety

The application is designed for safe practice:
- No real banking credentials are required.
- No actual financial transactions take place.
- No real bookings or food orders are submitted.
- Users can practise without risking money or personal information.
